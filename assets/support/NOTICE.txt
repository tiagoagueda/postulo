Both are ones Buy Me a Coffee publishes for people to use, and they are used here for what
they are for: linking to this project's own account.

buy-me-a-coffee.png   The banner, shipped unmodified. No longer shown; kept because links
                      to it exist outside this repository.

buy-me-a-coffee-qr.png  The QR code Buy Me a Coffee generated for this project's account,
                      scaled from its 3000px original to 330px and written out as RGB PNG.
                      Nothing else was changed: the code, its quiet zone and the cup in the
                      middle are theirs.

                      330 is not a round number. The code has little margin -- stylised
                      round modules with a logo over the centre -- and a strict decoder
                      reads it at 300, 330, 375 and 440 and fails at 360, 400, 500 and
                      above. 330 was chosen because it decoded under every resampling
                      tried. A phone is far more forgiving than a strict decoder, but
                      anything that regenerates or resizes this file should read it back
                      before committing it.

                      It resolves to https://www.buymeacoffee.com/tiagoagueda
