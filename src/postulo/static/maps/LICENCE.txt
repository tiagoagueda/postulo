Natural Earth — the world's outline under the map's dots (#108)

`world-outline.json` is the 1:110m country boundaries published by Natural Earth
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com): the polygons cut at the antimeridian the way an
equirectangular map is and simplified (Douglas-Peucker), written as closed rings of
latitude and longitude in degrees, the form Leaflet draws (#1153). A ring inside
another is a hole, so the file is drawn as one polygon with the even-odd rule.

Natural Earth's data are in the public domain (the project's terms of use at
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/): no licence is asked, and this
note is kept beside the file the way the flags keep theirs. The outline is a drawing
of the data, not a copy of it, and it is regenerated from the publication rather than
edited by hand.
