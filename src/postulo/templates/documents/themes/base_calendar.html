{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The Calendar printed (#342): the month, week, day or agenda that was on screen, set by a theme. **One template for the four views.** `calendar_print.as_context` hands each the same shape: the period's name, who printed it and when, the kinds shown and the search, ``days`` -- every day of the period with its entries -- and for a month and a week ``weeks``, the same days in rows of seven. This draws a month as a grid, and a week, a day and an agenda as days one under another; an agenda leaves out the days with nothing on, as the page does. **Every entry.** The screen's month holds two a day and counts the rest; paper holds them all. Nothing that is a control is here: no buttons, no legend, no navigation. **Plain values, and no model**, as for a report and a list (#340, #341). An entry is its kind, its two lines, its time or none for a whole day, and whether it is over -- a reminder done, an interview cancelled, a deadline already met -- which is struck through *and* said in words, because a printed page may be grey. **Every side is logical**, for the reason `base_report.html` gives, and the paper is Postulo's to write (#344). A month is drawn on its side unless somebody chose otherwise. {% endcomment %} {{ document_title }} {% block page_styles %}{% endblock %}
