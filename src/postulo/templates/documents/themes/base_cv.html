The space and the brackets are characters for the reason the separator is one, and the author in the file's properties stays the bare name: "Dr" is how somebody is addressed, not who wrote the document. **Each of the three is in a `` of its own.** They are three things somebody typed, and any of them may be in another script than the one beside it or than the document: left to the line, the full stop of "Dr." before an Arabic name is drawn on the far side of it (".Dr"), and that of an Arabic form of address before a Latin name on the wrong side of its letter. Isolated, each is read by itself and the brackets stay the line's. Only on a line that prints a form of address or pronouns: the name alone is written as it always was, so a CV that prints neither is the same bytes it was. **The telephone number is written left to right, whatever the document is** (#304).
