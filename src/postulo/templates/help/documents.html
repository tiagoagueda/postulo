{% blocktranslate trimmed %} CVs and portfolios are variants of your career record: each one chooses which entries to show, in which order, and how they are set. Correct an entry in Your career and every variant changes with it. Letters are the cover and motivation letters, with their own text. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Files are documents you kept as they came: a diploma, a certificate, a reference letter. They can be marked as the proof of an entry of your career, and sent with an application. Sent documents are copies of what went out, exactly as it went, kept unchanged so that you can see later what you claimed. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} A CV or a letter can be downloaded as the formats this instance offers, previewed beside its controls, and compared with an earlier version that was sent. {% endblocktranslate %}
