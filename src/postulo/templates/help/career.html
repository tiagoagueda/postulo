{% blocktranslate trimmed %} This is the master copy of what you have done: every role, every qualification, your skills and the rest, written once. A CV chooses from it and never copies it, so a title corrected here is corrected on every CV. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} The list on the left jumps to a section and shows how many entries it holds. Each section has its own Add, and each entry its own edit, copy in another language and delete. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Import reads a Europass CV; Export writes your details and your career as one file you can keep or move to another Postulo; Preview shows the whole record as a document, and Your CVs opens the variants made from it. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Answers keeps what application forms keep asking, such as why this company, your notice period or your salary expectation. On an application, What I answered keeps its own copy of what you told that employer, so changing a saved answer later never changes what was sent. Answers are never printed on a CV and never shared. {% endblocktranslate %}
