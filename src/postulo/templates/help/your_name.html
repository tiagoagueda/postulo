{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Your first and last name are the name your CVs and letters print at their top. Form of address and Pronouns are optional. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} A form of address is what is written before a name, and pronouns say how to refer to you. Nothing is assumed when either is blank, and neither is ever worked out from your name. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Each menu offers what is in use in the language of your career record, which is chosen under Contact block, or in the language you read Postulo in while that is blank. Choose Other… to write your own in the box that opens after the menu. A language Postulo has no list for offers Other… alone. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} What is kept is the text itself. If your career record changes language, a choice made from the other language's list stays what it was, and is shown as Other… with its text in the box. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Neither is printed anywhere unless a CV says so: each CV has a switch for the form of address and one for the pronouns under its own Settings. Letters print neither. {% endblocktranslate %}
