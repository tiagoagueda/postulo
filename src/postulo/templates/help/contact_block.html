{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The help of *Contact block* (#302), drawn by the card's drawer and by the page at /help/contact-block/. {% endcomment %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} The contact block is what a CV prints under your name, and each CV chooses which parts of it to print under its own Settings. A letter prints only your email address, your telephone number and where you are. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Headline is a short professional title, such as “Backend engineer”. A CV that has a headline of its own prints that one instead. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Location is where you are, as a document should print it. Left blank, your documents print the town and country of your primary postal address, never the street, and the box shows that line in grey. The blank is what is kept, so making another address the primary changes what is printed without this page being saved again. Type something and that is printed instead. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Language of your career record says which language your experience, education and projects are typed in. A CV in another language shows what you have translated and says what it could not. It also decides which forms of address and pronouns the menus of Your name offer. Left as it is, it follows the language you read Postulo in. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Your telephone numbers and your links have cards of their own further down. Where keeping several of one sort is switched off for you, this card has a single box for that sort instead. It holds the primary one: what you type there replaces it, and emptying the box removes it. The others recorded before are kept and not shown, and come back untouched if it is switched on again. {% endblocktranslate %}
