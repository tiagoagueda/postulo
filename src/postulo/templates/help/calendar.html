{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The help of the *Calendar* (#847), drawn by the page's drawer and by the page at /help/calendar/. {% endcomment %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Everything with a date is here: interviews, appointments with an adviser, reminders, an application's deadline, the day a listing closes and the day an offer must be answered. The key above the calendar names the colours, and each name switches that kind off and on. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Month, week, day and agenda are four views of the same days. Interviews are scheduled from an application's page; reminders and appointments are made here, on any day. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} The calendar can be downloaded as an iCalendar file, for the period and kinds on the screen, and a file from another calendar can be read in, with a review of what it holds before anything is added. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Under Subscriptions, a calendar somebody else keeps -- an employment office's appointments, a training provider's sessions -- can be followed by its address. Postulo reads it again on its own and draws its events in the colour you chose, marked as from it. They cannot be changed here; any of them can be added as an appointment or an interview of your own. {% endblocktranslate %}
