{% blocktranslate trimmed %} A row is one way of being reached on a messaging service: Matrix, XMPP, Signal, Telegram, Threema or another. {% blocktranslate trimmed %} A handle is shown as text and is never a link: a matrix.to or t.me address belongs to a third party, and Postulo opens and builds none that you did not ask for. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} A telephone number that is also on a messaging app belongs under Telephone numbers. The filled star marks the primary. The bin removes a row at once, after asking, without waiting for Save. {% endblocktranslate %}
