{% load i18n postulo %} {% comment %} Several telephone numbers for one holder, with the one to use marked. The primary is a radio rather than a checkbox per row, and the radios share one name outside the formset's prefixes, so the browser enforces "exactly one" before anything is posted. A checkbox per row can be ticked twice, and the invariant would then be something the server repairs after the fact rather than something that cannot be said. The radio's value is the row's form prefix and not its primary key, because somebody adding their first two numbers is choosing between rows that do not have keys yet. `remove_at_once` is *Your details* (#303): *Primary* is a star and *Remove* a bin that asks in a dialog and takes the row off there and then, and a row it took off comes back after a refused save as the hidden key it left. Without it -- the contact form -- the row keeps its *Remove* box, and a ticked one goes when the page is saved. On either page a row the table no longer holds -- posted from a copy of the page that is out of date -- is drawn as that key too. `min-w-0` on the fieldset: a fieldset is never narrower than the narrowest its content can be, which no other box insists on, and the country and the number in one group (#304) are together a little wider than a phone leaves. Let shrink, the group wraps. `card_help` is *Your details* as well (#302): the card has a question mark, whose one sentence is the one under the legend and whose drawer holds what the two paragraphs under the rows say. `tips` makes what a row's boxes are for each box's tooltip -- but not on the row a new number is typed into, whose sentence says what a + does and has to be read before a national number with no country is refused. `refused` keeps the card's sentence under the legend. Without them -- the contact form -- all of that stays in sight, as it was, and the template draws exactly what it drew before #302: each switch is written on the line of what it follows, so it leaves no line behind. The sentence under the legend is the topic's own summary, one string for both. {% endcomment %}
