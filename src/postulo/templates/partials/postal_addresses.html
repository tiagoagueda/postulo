A row reads in the order somebody decides it (#306, as #213 describes for a telephone number): what the address is -- its kind, and a name only while the kind is Other -- then the address, then the controls that act on the row. Source order is the reading order; below `sm` every box is a line of its own. The address's parts are drawn in the order its own country writes them, each called what that country calls it, in the language the reader is using: the order and the names follow the country on the row and the words follow the catalogue (#147). A country Postulo has no rules for gets the neutral words and the order they were always in, and so does every country while the rules are switched off for the reader. What a part's country refuses is said beside that part, in the part's own `.field`, with the id its box already names in `aria-describedby`. `remove_at_once` is *Your details* (#303), as in `phone_numbers.html`: a star, a bin, a dialog, and the row gone there and then. What a country's rules refuse stays under the rows either way: it says what may be typed, and it is the plugin's sentence, there only while the plugin acts for the reader. Each switch is written on the line of what it follows, so that without them the rows are drawn as before #302. {% endcomment %}
