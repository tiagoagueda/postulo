{% load i18n %} {% comment %} Two ways to read a CV or a letter, one at a time (#942): its **PDF** -- what is sent, page breaks and paper included -- and a **Quick view**, the HTML that follows the page in milliseconds. `……` **Without a script both are shown, one over the other**, and the strip of tabs stays `hidden`: a tab that does nothing would be a lie. The script (`[data-preview-tabs]` in `assets/js/app.js`) shows the strip, makes it an ARIA tablist (the tabs are real buttons, the panels are real regions) and hides the panel that is not chosen. Left and Right move between the tabs, Home and End go to the first and last, and only the chosen tab is in the Tab order. {% endcomment %}
