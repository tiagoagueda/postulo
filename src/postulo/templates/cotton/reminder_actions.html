Until #238 a reminder could be made and it could be ticked off, and that was all. A due time typed wrongly could only be ticked off and written again; a reminder that arrived at a bad moment could only be ticked off as though it had been dealt with. Both of those are the record being falsified to work around a missing button. **Done stays outside the menu** where there is room for it. It is what happens to almost every reminder, and burying the common action behind a trigger to make room for three rare ones is the trade the wrong way round. `compact` is for where there is no room -- a month's cell, a week's narrow column (#316) -- and there *Done* is the menu's first item rather than a second control squeezing the reminder's own words. **Later is two items and a link, not a date control.** *Tomorrow* and *next week* are what somebody pressing *Later* nearly always means, and they need no typing. A date box inside `role="menu"` is not a menu item, and a menu with a form field in the middle of it stops being read as a menu at all -- so a chosen time goes through *Another time*, which opens the edit form, where there is room for a date *and* an hour and where the field has a label. The reminder moves and is announced again either way; `postpone_reminder` is the one place that decides that. `next_url` travels with every action, the two links included, so that whatever was pressed brings the person back to the page it was pressed on -- the calendar, on the day and in the shape it was showing. `align` is the menu's own, for a menu that would open off the edge of a grid. {% endcomment %}
