The preview is the reason this page stopped being a loop over `form`. Somebody choosing between *Violet* and *Rose* for a label called "Dream job" is not choosing between two words, and a select full of colour names asks them to imagine the answer. Here every option draws itself, and the pill at the top is the actual tag: their word, the colour they have clicked, the icon they have clicked. It follows a click with no script at all -- `assets/css/app.css` matches the checked radio's value under `.tag-appearance` and paints the preview from it, which is why the wrapper class is on the card and not decoration. The preview carries all twelve icons and the stylesheet folds away the eleven that are not wanted; a page without CSS shows a pill with twelve icons in it, which is honest about being a preview, and the radios underneath work regardless. The name in the preview is the *saved* name, not what is being typed -- that part would need script, and a name is the one thing about a tag that needs no previewing. {% endcomment %}
