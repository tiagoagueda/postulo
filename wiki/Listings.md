# Listings

The stage before applications. Every posting you notice — captured from a page or typed
in — waits here until you decide what to do with it.

## Where things come from

- **Capture a posting.** Paste an address, or the page itself, and Postulo reads it. What
  it read appears at the top of the Listings page as a capture waiting for review; review
  it, correct anything the parser got wrong, and it becomes a listing. See
  [Capturing postings](Capturing-postings).
- **Add a listing.** Type it. The company and the title are enough to start — a listing
  can be thin, an application cannot — and the rest can be filled in later.

Both land in the same place, marked **New**.

## Deciding

Each listing has three doors:

- **Shortlist** — worth coming back to. It stays in the to-decide list, flagged.
- **Discard** — not this one. You can say why (not for me, the pay, the location, closed,
  other), and the reason is kept: what you passed on, and why, is part of the record.
  A discarded listing can be restored.
- **Apply** — the decision that matters. This records the application — status, how
  you applied, priority, your own deadline, tags — and the timeline begins. The listing
  leaves the to-decide list and shows as **Applied** from then on.

Two states are never set by hand, because they follow from other facts: **Applied**,
once an application exists, and **Closed**, once the opening is gone or its closing
date has passed.

## The filters

**To decide** is what you see by default: new and shortlisted listings that are still
open. The other filters — Shortlisted, Discarded, Applied, Closed, Everything — are one
click away, each with its count.

## Closing dates

A listing with a closing date is ordered by it in the to-decide list, and the dashboard
says how many close this week.

## Already applied?

Recording after the fact is the common case. Two shortcuts keep it a single step:

- When reviewing a capture, tick **I have already applied to this one** and the listing
  becomes an application straight away, marked applied today.
- **Record an application**, on the dashboard and the Applications page, is the one-form
  path: it creates the listing and the application together. Underneath it is exactly
  those two steps, so the record is the same whichever door you used.

## What Insights makes of it

Insights reports how many listings you noticed, how many you applied to, and how many
you discarded — your selectivity, and the first number that says whether the postings
you find are the postings you want.
